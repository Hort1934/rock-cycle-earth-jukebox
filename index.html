How sight becomes sound

1 · Scan

Every frame is averaged down to a small grid (about 72 rows; ultra-wide hyperwall frames get up to 320 columns). A playhead crosses it in 32 steps, so one sweep takes two bars. For flat maps, left to right is west to east.

2 · Separate

Pixels are sorted into layers by the color rules of the visualization's own color bar: dust is red, sea salt is blue, warm anomalies are red, and so on.

3 · Map

Value or height → pitch, kept in a musical mode. Amount on screen → loudness. Position → stereo. Sudden appearance → percussion. Motion and scene tension → the low drone.

4 · Style

Genres only swap instruments and the room. Pitch, rhythm and loudness always come from the data. The optional groove is the only sound that is not data.
